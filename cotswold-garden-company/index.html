Why customers choose us

Founded in 2005 by Simon Dowle, a qualified civil engineer with ten years' experience designing and building multi-million pound construction projects. His insistence on the best workmanship, the finest materials and plants runs through every garden we make.

In our first three years as BALI members we won four national awards for our gardens, including a 2016 Principal Award.

Working throughout the Cotswolds: Cirencester, Tetbury, Cheltenham, Burford and beyond.
