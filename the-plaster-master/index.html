What we do

Smooth walls and ceilings, from one room to the whole house

Tired of looking at that old artex ceiling, or pebbledash that's gone a bit bald? Whatever the job, you get a smooth, sound finish ready for decorating, and a house left as tidy as we found it.

  • Reskims

    A fresh skim coat over tired, cracked or uneven walls and ceilings.

  • Artex ceilings

    Old artex replaced with a modern, flat plastered ceiling.

  • Reboarding and overboarding

    Blown or water-damaged ceilings taken back and boarded again.

  • Extensions and conservatories

    New rooms boarded, dot-and-dabbed and plastered to finish.

  • Rendering and pebbledash

    Exterior walls stripped back, scratch-coated and re-rendered.

  • Repairs and renovations

    Patching after old tiles, dado rails or leaks, through to full renovations.

About us

Meet Mark, The Plaster Master

Mark Pannell started in the trade in the early 1980s, labouring for a local plasterer before moving onto the tools. Today The Plaster Master is a small team of two plasterers, offering a reliable service at a competitive price.

Much of the work comes from people who have used us before, and from their friends and family. Planning a bigger project? Just ask and we'll put you in touch with bricklayers, plumbers, carpenters, decorators, tilers and kitchen fitters.
