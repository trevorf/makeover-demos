What we do

Every part of the garden build

We would consider any project regardless of size, shape, access and budget.

Paving and terraces

Natural stone terraces and paths, including Yorkstone and limestone, with coping to brick walls.

Walls and brickwork

Set-out, excavation, concrete foundations, retaining walls and brickwork.

Bespoke timber work

Pergolas, arches, fencing and gates, including acoustic fencing to road-side boundaries.

Lawns and planting

Soil preparation, imported topsoil and compost, turf and seed, tree and hedge planting.

Drainage and driveways

Drainage works and new drives as part of the wider landscape.

Water, lighting and irrigation

Water features, garden lighting and irrigation, plus wrought iron railings.

How we work

Planned properly, start to finish

  1. Setting out the project

    A full bill of quantities and breakdown for the proposed works, working closely with the garden designer and you, so the job starts and finishes on time and on budget.

  2. Programme and scheduling

    A programme of works showing the build process and the key dates for deliveries of landscape materials, alongside any other trades on site.

  3. Access and location

    Years of building gardens in London mean we know how to work with restricted access, and how to complete a build without making a mess.

  4. Specialist contractors

    A network of trusted specialist contractors we call on to achieve that perfect landscape or feature.

About us

Landscape design is our passion

Director Lee White has been associated with the landscape industry for more than 30 years, working with some of the best landscape construction companies, garden designers and landscape architects in London and the home counties.

Lee is still very much hands on. The team works closely with garden designers and manages every aspect of the build, including any planning issues and liaison with the local authorities. Private clients with a design in mind, or a project that needs some fine tuning, are just as welcome.
