What we do

Domestic heating, plumbing and gas appliances

No heating job is too large or too small. We'll look at your system, explain the options for your home and budget, and give you a no-obligation quote.

Boiler installation and replacement

A-rated condensing combi and system boilers, fitted by Gas Safe engineers with a fixed-price quote. We fit all quality brands and prefer Worcester Bosch.

Boiler servicing and breakdowns

Yearly servicing keeps your boiler reliable, efficient and safe, and we repair breakdowns on all types of central heating system.

Gas fires, cookers and hobs

Installation, servicing and annual safety checks. Our gas fire service includes a leak test, full strip and clean, pressure and safety-device tests and a flue check.

Power flushing

Cold spots in the middle of your radiators? A power flush clears sludge and scale so your heating warms up properly and costs less to run.

Plumbing and bathrooms

All aspects of plumbing, from leaks to complete bathroom installations, starting with a free, no-obligation visit to talk through your plans.

Appliance installation

Washing machines, dishwashers and other white goods, including American-style fridges, connected properly. We can take the old machine away too.
