Welcome to A+B Fencing

Your local fencing specialists

We’re a Cotswold-based, family-run showroom, workshop and timber supplies facility, established for over 35 years.

Our highly skilled local tradesmen, carpenters and installation teams handle every kind of job: new installations, bespoke garden projects, or repairs to existing fences and structures.

Not after an installation? Trade and domestic customers can buy timber, materials and fixings direct from us, with delivery available where required.
