Heating system conversion

Old back boiler out. New Worcester Bosch combi in.

A recent job from start to finish: an open-vented system with a back boiler and gas fire, replaced with a sealed Worcester Bosch combination boiler that now lives in the airing cupboard.

  1. 1Gas fire, back boiler and hot water cylinder removed
  2. 2Pipework converted from one-pipe to a more efficient two-pipe system
  3. 3Radiators replaced, with new thermostatic valves on each
  4. 4New 28mm copper gas supply run from the meter and tested
